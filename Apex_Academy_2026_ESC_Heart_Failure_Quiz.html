ESC 2026 • Table 4 & Figures 1–3

Redefined HF Phenotypes, Stages (A–D) & Therapy Pyramid

HFmrEF has been eliminated. HFrEF now spans LVEF <50%, and HFpEF spans LVEF ≥50%.

2026 REDEFINED HFrEF

HFrEF: LVEF < 50%

Includes all symptomatic patients with LVEF up to 49% (HFmrEF removed).

Class I Foundational Medical Therapy (FMT):
• ACE-I / ARNI (or ARB if intolerant) — Class I, A
• Beta-blocker (bisoprolol, carvedilol, metoprolol CR/XL, nebivolol) — Class I, A
• Steroidal MRA (spironolactone, eplerenone) — Class I, A
• SGLT2-I (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin) — Class I, A
2026 REDEFINED HFpEF

HFpEF: LVEF ≥ 50%

Requires LVEF ≥50% (never previously <50%) + structural/functional abnormality + raised NPs.

Class I Foundational Medical Therapy (FMT):
• SGLT2-I (dapagliflozin or empagliflozin) — Class I, A
• MRA (steroidal MRA or non-steroidal finerenone) — Class I, A
• ACE-I/ARB/ARNI may be considered to reduce HFH — Class IIb, C
Figure 3 • New Nomenclature for HF Therapies (FMT + AMT = GDMT; Devices/Procedures = GDIT)
1. Foundational Medical Therapy (FMT)

Class I therapies for unselected HF patients with proven reduction in mortality and/or HFH. Uptitrate every 1–2 weeks (Class I) and continue lifelong even if LVEF improves (Class I).

2. Additional Medical Therapy (AMT)

Loop diuretics (Class I), IV iron (Class I for QoL, IIa for HFH), Semaglutide/Tirzepatide in BMI ≥30 & LVEF ≥45% (Class IIa), Digoxin/Digitoxin in LVEF ≤40% (Class IIa), Ivabradine (Class IIa), Vericiguat in LVEF <45% (Class IIb).

3. Guideline-Directed Interventional Therapy (GDIT)

ICD (Class I ischaemic, IIa non-ischaemic), CRT (Class I if LBBB ≥150 ms), Mitral TEER in severe secondary MR (upgraded to Class I, B1), SAVR/TAVI (Class I), LVAD/Transplant (Class I), AF/AVN ablation (Class IIa).

ESC 2026 • Figures 11–15

Decompensated Heart Failure (DHF) & Natriuresis-Guided Decongestion

DHF replaces the term "Acute HF". Rule-out cut-offs: NT-proBNP <300 pg/mL, BNP <100 pg/mL, MR-proANP <120 pg/mL.

Figure 13 • Immediate Trigger Identification: "CHAMPIT"

Coronary syndrome (acute) • Hypertensive emergency • Arrhythmias • Mechanical causes • Pulmonary embolism • Infections/Inflammation • Tamponade.

Figure 15 • Stepwise I.V. Diuretic & Spot Urinary Sodium Algorithm

1. Start I.V. Loop Diuretic (Class I, A): Give 40 mg i.v. furosemide if diuretic-naïve, OR double (2×) the daily oral dose i.v. in chronic loop diuretic users.

2. Check Early Diuretic Response: Measure Spot urinary Na+ (uNa+) at 2 h ≥ 70 mmol/L OR Urine output at 6 h ≥ 100 mL/h (Class IIb, B1).

3. If Insufficient Response: Double i.v. loop diuretic dose AND/OR add short-term i.v. acetazolamide (500 mg o.d. — ADVOR) or eGFR-based oral hydrochlorothiazide (25–100 mg — CLOROTIC) (Class IIa, B1).

4. Parallel Disease-Modifying Therapy: Initiate in-hospital SGLT2-I after initial stabilization (Class I, B1) and implement rapid pre-discharge + 6-week post-discharge FMT uptitration (STRONG-HF, Class I, B2).

Figure 14 • Pre-Discharge Decongestion Targets (Exclude Residual Congestion — Class I, C)
Clinical: Congestion score = 0 (optimal) or 1–2 (acceptable); NYHA Class ≤ II.
Laboratory: ΔNPs ≥30% drop, OR NT-proBNP <1500 pg/mL, OR BNP <250 pg/mL.
Imaging: E/e′ <10 (optimal) or <15; IVC <21 mm & >50% collapse; Lung US <5 B-lines.
@Apex High-Yield Summary

2026 ESC Heart Failure Rapid-Fire Pearls

Key paradigm shifts between the 2021/2023 and 2026 ESC Heart Failure Guidelines.

🫀

Goodbye HFmrEF & New Therapy Terms

  • Two LVEF Phenotypes Only: HFmrEF is eliminated. HFrEF = LVEF <50%; HFpEF = LVEF ≥50%.
  • FMT, AMT & GDIT: GDMT is split into Foundational Medical Therapy (FMT) and Additional Medical Therapy (AMT); devices/interventions are GDIT.
  • Universal Class I Pillars: Both SGLT2-Is and MRAs (including non-steroidal finerenone in HFpEF) are Class I, Level A across all LVEF!
💊

GLP-1/GIP, Digitoxin & Amyloidosis

  • Obesity + HF: Semaglutide or tirzepatide is Class IIa, B1 for symptomatic HF with LVEF ≥45% and BMI ≥30 kg/m² regardless of diabetes.
  • DIGIT-HF Upgrade: Digoxin or hepatically cleared digitoxin upgraded to Class IIa, B1 in symptomatic HFrEF with LVEF ≤40%.
  • ATTR Amyloidosis (NYHA I–III): TTR silencer (vutrisiran) or stabilizer (tafamidis / acoramidis) is Class I, Level A.
⚡

Mitral TEER, DanGer Shock & AF

  • Secondary MR: Mitral TEER is upgraded to Class I, Level B1 in stable HFrEF with persistent severe secondary MR despite optimal FMT + CRT.
  • STEMI Cardiogenic Shock: Multidisciplinary Shock Team (Class I, C) + microaxial flow pump in selected STEMI-CS without hypoxic brain injury risk (Class IIa, B1). Unselected MCS or routine IABP is Class III.
  • CHA2DS2-VA Score: Replaces CHA2DS2-VASc (sex category removed; OAC recommended if score ≥2, should be considered if score = 1).
🩸

Iron Deficiency, CKD & Pregnancy

  • Redefined Iron Deficiency: TSAT <20% has higher sensitivity/specificity for true ID and better predicts IV iron response.
  • Acceptable Creatinine Rise: A transient rise in creatinine <50% above baseline (as long as eGFR >15 mL/min/1.73 m²) after starting FMT is acceptable—do NOT stop FMT!
  • Pregnancy: Switch non-selective beta-blockers (carvedilol) to beta1-selective blockers (metoprolol, bisoprolol) (Class I, C); stop ACE-I/ARB/ARNI, MRA, SGLT2-I, and ivabradine (Class III).
