Diagnostic Pathways (PERC & YEARS)

  • Low Pretest (<15%) + PERC Negative: Exclude PE clinically without D-dimer or imaging.
  • YEARS Algorithm: 0 YEARS items → D-dimer <1000 ng/mL rules out PE; ≥1 YEARS item → cutoff <500 ng/mL (or age-adjusted).
  • High Pretest (>50%): Skip D-dimer; proceed directly to CTPA + empirical anticoagulation.
  • Pregnancy + DVT Signs: Bilateral leg compression ultrasound first to avoid fetal radiation.

Anticoagulation & Critical Care

  • Parenteral: LMWH preferred over UFH unless severe CKD or imminent procedure/lysis.
  • Oral: DOACs Class 1 over VKAs (including most cancer PE); warfarin mandatory in triple-positive APS; LMWH in pregnancy.
  • Hemodynamics: Norepinephrine first-line; avoid excess fluids; use HFNC and avoid intubation if possible.
  • CTEPD/CTEPH: Screen at every visit for ≥1 year; V/Q scan is the most sensitive screening test.

Pearl 1 • Normotensive Shock (D2)

Normal BP with elevated lactate, oliguria, or mottling is Category D2 (incipient cardiopulmonary failure)—not benign intermediate risk—requiring ICU care and urgent PERT evaluation.

Pearl 2 • Avoid Peri-Intubation Arrest

Positive-pressure ventilation and vasodilatory induction agents collapse RV preload. Favor HFNC; if intubation is unavoidable, use hemodynamically neutral agents (e.g., ketamine).

Pearl 3 • VA-ECMO in Category E2

VA-ECMO (not VV-ECMO or IABP) is Class 2a in refractory shock/arrest as a bridge to mechanical thrombectomy, surgical embolectomy, or RV recovery.

Pearl 4 • 1-Year CTEPH Surveillance

Screen for persistent dyspnea/functional limitation at every visit for ≥1 year post-PE; order a V/Q scan if CTEPH is suspected.
