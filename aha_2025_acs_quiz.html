2025 Antiplatelet & Anticoagulation Pathways

  • Ticagrelor Monotherapy (Class 1): Transition to ticagrelor monotherapy (stop aspirin) ≥1 month post-PCI in patients who tolerated DAPT to reduce bleeding.
  • Prasugrel Contraindication: Class 3 Harm in any patient with prior stroke or TIA; also Class 3 Harm upstream before anatomy is known.
  • ACS + OAC (e.g., AF): Stop aspirin 1–4 weeks post-PCI; continue DOAC + P2Y12 inhibitor (clopidogrel preferred).
  • GI Protection: PPI is Class 1 during DAPT in patients at elevated GI bleeding risk.

Procedural PCI & Cardiogenic Shock

  • Intravascular Imaging (Class 1): Upgraded to Class 1 (LOE A) for IVUS/OCT guidance in complex ACS PCI.
  • Radial Access (Class 1): Preferred over femoral to lower major bleeding and mortality.
  • Complete vs. Culprit-Only: Complete revascularization is Class 1 in stable STEMI/NSTE-ACS, whereas routine immediate non-culprit PCI in AMI-CS is Class 3 Harm (CULPRIT-SHOCK).
  • Shock MCS: Microaxial flow pump (Impella) is Class 2a in selected STEMI-CS (DanGer Shock); routine IABP or routine VA-ECMO is Class 3 No Benefit.

Lipids, Anemia & Secondary Prevention

  • LDL-C Intensification: High-intensity statin (Class 1). Recheck lipids in 4–8 weeks: add non-statin if LDL-C ≥70 mg/dL (Class 1) or 55–69 mg/dL (Class 2a).
  • Anemia (MINT Trial): RBC transfusion to maintain Hb ≥10 g/dL may be reasonable (Class 2b) in anemic ACS without active bleeding.
  • Colchicine: Low-dose 0.5 mg daily may be reasonable (Class 2b) to reduce recurrent MACE.
  • Cardiac Rehab: Outpatient Class 1; home-based cardiac rehab is a Class 2a alternative.

Pearl 1 • IVUS/OCT Now Class 1

Intracoronary imaging to guide complex ACS PCI moved from Class 2a to Class 1 (LOE A) based on RENOVATE-COMPLEX-PCI, OCTOBER, and ILUMIEN IV.

Pearl 2 • DanGer Shock (Impella)

Microaxial flow pump is Class 2a in selected STEMI-associated cardiogenic shock (excluding comatose/anoxic arrest), while routine IABP and routine VA-ECMO are Class 3 No Benefit.

Pearl 3 • MINT Transfusion Shift

Liberal transfusion targeting Hb ≥10 g/dL in non-bleeding anemic ACS patients is newly supported as Class 2b—shifting away from rigid Hb <7 g/dL thresholds in AMI.

Pearl 4 • Pharmacoinvasive Window

If anticipated FMC-to-device >120 min in STEMI (<12h onset), give fibrinolysis within 30 min, then transfer for routine angiography within 2–24 hours (or immediate rescue PCI if failed).
