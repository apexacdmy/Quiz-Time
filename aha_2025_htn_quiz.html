2025 Risk-Based Initiation & Goals

Stage 1 HTN (130–139 / 80–89)

Start medication immediately if clinical CVD, DM, CKD, or PREVENT 10-yr CVD risk ≥7.5%. If <7.5%, start medication if BP remains ≥130/80 after 3–6 mo lifestyle therapy.

Stage 2 HTN (≥140 / ≥90)

Start 2 first-line agents of different classes—preferring a single-pill fixed-dose combination (Class 1). Race-based drug selection is removed.

Treatment Targets

Universal goal <130/80 mmHg; actively encouraged to pursue SBP <120 mmHg in higher-risk adults if tolerated.

Routine Baseline Workup

Now includes routine spot UACR alongside BMP, lipids, HbA1c, and ECG. Avoid cuffless smartwatches for diagnosis or titration.

Primary Aldosteronism & Resistant HTN

  • Expanded PA Screening: Screen all Stage 2 and resistant HTN patients with PAC/PRA regardless of normal serum potassium. Hold MRAs ≥4 weeks prior.
  • True Resistant HTN: Confirm adherence + out-of-office BP (ABPM/HBPM) on ≥3 optimal agents (including a diuretic).
  • 4th-Line Agent: Spironolactone 25 mg daily is preferred (PATHWAY-2) when eGFR & K+ allow.
  • Renal Denervation: Class 2b cautious option after multidisciplinary evaluation and shared decision-making.

Acute Vascular & Pregnancy Thresholds

  • Severe HTN (>180/120, no TOD): Replaces "hypertensive urgency." Manage outpatient with oral therapy; avoid IV agents.
  • Acute ICH (SBP 150–220): Smoothly lower SBP to 130–140 mmHg (avoiding steep drops or SBP <110).
  • Aortic Dissection: IV beta-blocker FIRST (HR <60), then IV vasodilator to SBP 100–120 within 20 min.
  • Pregnancy Chronic HTN (CHAP): Treat at ≥140/90 mmHg with labetalol or ER nifedipine; early postpartum surveillance + lifelong CVD risk flagging.

Top 2025 AHA/ACC Hypertension Paradigm Shifts
Guideline ↗
Pearl 1 • Race-Free PREVENT

PREVENT replaces Pooled Cohort Equations (10-year total CVD ≥7.5% triggers immediate Stage 1 therapy), and race-based antihypertensive drug selection is eliminated.

Pearl 2 • Dementia Prevention

Intensive BP lowering (<130/80 and <120 when tolerated) is explicitly affirmed to reduce mild cognitive impairment and dementia (SPRINT-MIND).

Pearl 3 • Potassium Salt Substitutes

Endorsed for home-cooked sodium reduction (SSaSS) EXCEPT in CKD or patients on K+-sparing medications. Optimal sodium goal <1500 mg/day; zero alcohol is safest.

Pearl 4 • CKD Creatinine Bump

Continue ACEi/ARB if serum creatinine rises ≤30% acutely after initiation—this reflects beneficial reduction in intraglomerular pressure.
