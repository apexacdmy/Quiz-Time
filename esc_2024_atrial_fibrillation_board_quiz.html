AF-CARE Integrated Management Framework

[C] Comorbidity & Risk Factor Control

Placed FIRST. BP target 120–129/70–79 mmHg, ≥10% weight loss, alcohol ≤3 drinks/week (≤30 g), optimal HF GDMT (SGLT2i), diabetes & OSA management.

[A] Avoid Stroke & Thromboembolism

Use sex-neutral CHA₂DS₂-VA (≥2 Class I; 1 Class IIa). DOACs preferred over VKAs (except mechanical valves & mod-severe MS). Manage modifiable bleeding risks.

[R] Reduce Symptoms (Rate & Rhythm)

Lenient initial rate <110 bpm. First-line catheter ablation is Class I in symptomatic paroxysmal AF. Early rhythm control (<12 mo) is Class IIa.

[E] Evaluation & Dynamic Reassessment

Mandatory structured reassessment at 6 months post-presentation and at least annually thereafter for renal function, stroke/bleeding risk, and adherence.

2024 Cardioversion & Catheter Ablation Rules

  • 24-Hour Safe Window (New): Early cardioversion without 3 weeks OAC or TEE is now restricted to AF duration ≤24 hours (shortened from 48h).
  • First-Line Paroxysmal Ablation: Class I (LOE A) after shared decision-making to reduce symptoms and recurrence.
  • HFrEF & Tachycardia-Induced CMP: Class I for catheter ablation to reverse LV systolic dysfunction.
  • Post-Ablation OAC: Continue OAC for ≥2 months in all patients, then indefinitely per CHA₂DS₂-VA regardless of sinus rhythm.

Top 2024 ESC AF Paradigm Shifts & Resident Pearls
Guideline ↗
Pearl 1 • CHA₂DS₂-VA

Female sex was removed from stroke risk scoring. OAC is Class I at CHA₂DS₂-VA ≥2 and Class IIa at 1. HCM and cardiac amyloidosis mandate OAC irrespective of score (Class I).

Pearl 2 • Surgical LAAO (LAAOS III)

Concomitant surgical LAA closure during cardiac surgery in AF patients is Class I as an adjunct to—not a substitute for—ongoing oral anticoagulation.

Pearl 3 • Breakthrough Stroke

Do NOT add antiplatelets to OAC (Class III) and do NOT routinely switch between DOACs empirically after breakthrough stroke—investigate adherence, dosing, and non-AF etiologies.

Pearl 4 • FRAIL-AF & Subclinical AF

In frail elderly stable on VKA (TTR ≥70%), continuing VKA may be considered over switching to DOAC (Class IIb). For device-detected AHRE, DOACs are Class IIb if high stroke/low bleed risk.

Pearl 5 • Bleeding Risk Scores

Never withhold or withdraw OAC based on HAS-BLED alone (Class III). Actively treat modifiable bleeding risks (HTN, NSAIDs, alcohol).
